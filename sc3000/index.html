City files (.sc3) for the game "SimCity 3000" lost with "www.sc3000.com"   Aug 5, 2026 updated

 

The uploader "www.sc3000.com" for the game "SimCity 3000" was shut down around 2019. The upladed city files were migratedto Simtropolis, but not all of them.

I collected the missing files from the Internet Archive. I verified that there were no duplicates by checking the MD5 hashes.

Download: web_archive_org.zip (179 MB)

 

Later, another person uploaded his own collection on https://community.simtropolis.com/forums/topic/764313-another-file-archive/. I copied remaining missing files.

Download: addition.zip (136MB)
